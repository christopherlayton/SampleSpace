# Critical Forecast

Six predictions about the numbers behind every test.

| Function | Gives |
|---|---|
| `qt(p, df)` | the *t* value with proportion *p* below it (critical values) |
| `pt(q, df)` | the area of the *t* distribution below *q* (p-values) |
| `pt(q, df, lower.tail = FALSE)` | the area above *q* |
| `power.t.test(n, delta, sd, sig.level)` | power, or whichever argument you leave out |
